What We Find

What DLX can find inside your customer list

Every business has a different mix. Here are the most common revenue opportunities we surface across the industries we work with.

🔁

Customers due for repeat work

Annual services, seasonal treatments, regular appointments — customers on a natural cycle who have not been reminded at the right time.

📋

Old quotes that need chasing

Quotes sent and forgotten. A well-timed follow-up — personalised, not spammy — closes a significant percentage that would otherwise go cold for good.

😴

Customers who bought once and vanished

First-time customers who never came back. These are among the easiest wins — they already trust you, they just need a prompt.

🌡️

Seasonal reminder campaigns

Spring lawn care. Winter boiler checks. Summer air-con regassing. Pre-summer colour refresh. Your service calendar already tells you when to reach out.

🔧

Declined work that needs a follow-up

A customer said no to a repair or treatment last time. That reason may no longer apply. A safety-focused reminder reopens the conversation naturally.

⭐

Review & referral moments

Happy customers who just completed a job are your best source of reviews and referrals. Most businesses never ask at the right moment.

💎

High-value customers worth prioritising

Customers with high lifetime value, high frequency, or expensive service history who have gone quiet are worth far more than a cold prospect.

📈

Upsell opportunities based on history

A customer's previous services, issues, and spending patterns tell you exactly what they are likely to need next. We make those connections automatically.
