Compliance & Data

Built Responsibly

All systems are built with UK GDPR, data protection law, and messaging compliance in mind.

🔒

UK GDPR Compliant

All data handling follows UK GDPR requirements. We never collect more data than is needed for the system to function, and we never share it with third parties for marketing.

📋

Consent-Based Messaging

All SMS and email outreach is based on existing customer relationships and prior consent. We do not support cold outreach to contacts without a lawful basis for communication.

🚫

What Our Systems Don't Do

Our systems do not provide medical, legal, or financial advice. They do not handle sensitive personal data such as payment card details, medical histories, or legal matter details.

🗄️

Data Retention

Call transcripts and recordings are automatically deleted after 60 days by default (configurable). All data is processed securely and stored in compliant EU/UK infrastructure.

🆘

Emergency Routing

If a caller mentions a medical or legal emergency, our AI Receptionist will never attempt to handle it. It immediately directs the caller to call 999 or the appropriate emergency service.

📝

Transparent & Logged

Every interaction is logged for dispute resolution and quality assurance. Callers are notified that calls may be recorded at the start of every AI Receptionist call.
